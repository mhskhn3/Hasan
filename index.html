About Me

HK

I am a Data Insights Specialist with 1.9+ years of experience driving operational efficiency and strategic decision-making for the Google xWS Onboarding & Vendor Management Project at Cognizant. My expertise lies in transforming raw, complex data into actionable insights through advanced SQL, data visualization, and automation. I design and maintain 10+ high-impact dashboards in PLX and Looker, collaborating directly with Google's Analytics Leadership and internal BI teams to ensure alignment with business goals.

What I bring to the table:
1. Data Engineering & Analytics: Proficient in SQL, Python (Pandas, NumPy), and statistical modeling to extract, clean, and analyze large datasets.
2. Visualization & Storytelling: Expert in Looker, PLX, Tableau, and Power BI—creating dashboards that drive decisions, not just display numbers.
3. Business Acumen: I don't just build reports; I answer the "why." I translate data into narratives that help leadership make confident, informed choices.
4. Automation & Machine Learning: Experienced in building NLP models, sentiment analysis, and predictive analytics to solve real-world business challenges.
