    About Me

    I'm Mohd Hasan Khan

    Since starting out in Mathematics, I've been drawn to the story hiding inside data. What began as curiosity through my post-graduate degree in Data Science turned into a career built around one question: what does this number actually mean for the business?

    Today I work as a Data Insight Specialist at Cognizant, sitting directly with Google's Analytics Leadership on the xWS Onboarding & Vendor Management program. I design and maintain 10+ high-impact dashboards in PLX and Looker — but the part I enjoy most isn't the query, it's turning a wall of numbers into a decision someone can act on.

    Along the way I've also worked with Beats By Dre on consumer insight, interned in machine learning at IIIT Bhubaneswar, and kept building side projects in deep learning and NLP — because the best way to really understand a concept is to build it yourself.
